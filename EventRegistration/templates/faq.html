Common Inquiries & Answers

Browse through answers to common questions about using the Eventure system.

Once logged into your Participant Dashboard, navigate to the **Browse Events** section on the sidebar. Under the active events, click the **Register** button, check your details (name, email, phone) in the registration form, and submit.

The **Watchlist** allows you to bookmark events you are interested in but not ready to register for. By adding an event to your watchlist, it will be tracked under **My Watchlist** on your dashboard, giving you a quick shortcut to register later.

After an event has concluded, the system administrator will grade participant performance or attendance and add specific remarks. You can view these grades in the **My Evaluation Marks** section of the sidebar.

You can toggle the primary dark mode using the **Moon / Sun toggle button** next to the logout button in the header dashboard. Additionally, you can choose from 9 custom curated themes using the **Select Theme** panel located at the bottom of the sidebar.

If you need to make changes to your registered name, phone, or email, navigate to the **Contact Us** page and send a support request. Admin staff will resolve it directly.
